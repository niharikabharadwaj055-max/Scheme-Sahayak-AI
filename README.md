# 🇮🇳 Scheme Sahayak AI – Government Scheme Recommendation System

## 📌 Project Overview

**Scheme Sahayak AI** is an AI-powered government scheme recommendation platform designed to help citizens discover government welfare schemes and benefits based on their personal details, eligibility criteria, and requirements.

Finding relevant government schemes can be challenging due to a lack of awareness, complex eligibility conditions, language barriers, and scattered information.

Scheme Sahayak AI aims to simplify this process by providing a centralized platform where users can explore relevant Central and State Government schemes through an accessible and user-friendly interface.

The platform provides personalized scheme recommendations, eligibility information, scheme descriptions, and multilingual assistance to make government welfare information more accessible.

## 🎯 Objectives

- To create awareness about Central and State Government schemes.
- To recommend relevant schemes based on user-provided information.
- To simplify complex eligibility criteria.
- To provide government scheme information in multiple languages.
- To improve accessibility for rural and underserved communities.
- To reduce the difficulty of finding suitable government benefits.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Personalized Scheme Recommendations
- Collects relevant user information.
- Identifies schemes matching the user's requirements.
- Provides recommendations based on eligibility conditions.

### 2. Central and State Government Schemes
- Provides information about various government welfare schemes.
- Covers different categories of beneficiaries.
- Helps users explore relevant government benefits.

### 3. AI-Powered Assistant
- Allows users to ask questions about government schemes.
- Provides simplified explanations of scheme details.
- Helps users understand eligibility requirements and benefits.

### 4. Multilingual Support
- Designed to support multiple Indian languages.
- Aims to improve accessibility for users from different linguistic backgrounds.
- Supports regional-language interaction.

### 5. Eligibility Checker
- Helps users understand whether they meet the eligibility requirements of a scheme.
- Uses user-provided details to identify potential eligibility.
- Displays relevant scheme information.

### 6. User-Friendly Interface
- Simple and accessible user interface.
- Easy navigation between scheme categories.
- Designed with rural and first-time internet users in mind.
